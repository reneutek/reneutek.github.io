About Reneutek

Modern engineering for aging and distressed structures.

We bring modern engineering solutions to existing buildings and infrastructure, with a commitment to technical excellence and sustainable practices—helping preserve both heritage and utility.

Our focus is on extending service life, enhancing structural safety, providing environmental protection and supporting the long-term usability of existing structures.
Our Mission
Innovative, cost-effective and durable solutions for structural safety, environmental protection and long-term usability.
What We Do

Our Services

01 / STRUCTURAL

Retrofitting of Structures

  • Seismic retrofitting for earthquake resistance
  • Load capacity enhancement for commercial and residential buildings
  • FRP wrapping and jacketing techniques
  • Foundation strengthening and underpinning
  • Structural health monitoring and evaluation
02 / REPAIR

Structural Rehabilitation

  • Restoration of damaged concrete and steel structures
  • Crack repair and stabilization
  • Corrosion protection and control
  • Rebar treatment and protective coatings
  • Rehabilitation of heritage buildings with minimal intervention
03 / PROTECTION

Waterproofing Solutions

  • Terrace, basement and roof waterproofing
  • Expansion joint sealing and repair
  • Injection grouting — PU, epoxy and cementitious
  • External facade and podium waterproofing
  • Industrial and wet area leak-proofing
04 / FINISHES

Flooring, Cladding & Painting

  • Industrial grade hardened and epoxy flooring
  • Domestic flooring — epoxy, tiled and hardened
  • Acid & Alkali proof tile work
  • Moisture & Heat proof Exterior Coatings
